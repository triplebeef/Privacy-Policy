# Reelo Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 16, 2026

At Reelo, your privacy matters. We believe you should have control over your recordings and personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Reelo handles, how we use it, and what stays on your device.

## 1. Your Recordings Stay Private

Reelo is designed to keep your recordings and personal content on your computer.

Depending on the features you use, Reelo may access:

- Your screen or application windows
- System audio and microphone input
- Webcam video
- Media files you choose to import or edit

**Your recordings, screenshots, audio, video, and related content are stored locally on your device.** Reelo does not automatically upload or share this content with us or third parties.

You choose what to record, where to save it, and whether to share it.

## 2. AI Features and Local Processing

Reelo offers features such as automatic subtitles and AI-generated summaries.

These features process your content directly on your device. Your recordings, transcripts, and generated summaries are not sent to external AI services for processing.

Some features may require downloading additional resources. These downloads involve connecting to external services but do not upload your personal recordings or media.

## 3. Information We Collect

Reelo stores certain information locally to remember your preferences and ensure the application works correctly. This may include your settings, selected devices, saved file locations, and troubleshooting information.

### Anonymous and Limited Diagnostics

Reelo automatically collects certain information, to help us improve the application and experience.

These reports may include:

- Application and Windows version information
- General hardware and performance information
- How frequently certain features are used
- Recording success rates and error information
- General recording durations and selected preferences

Diagnostic reports are intended to help us understand performance, resolve technical problems, and improve the overall user experience.

**Diagnostic reports will not include:**

- Your recordings, screenshots, audio, or video
- Transcripts, subtitles, or AI-generated summaries
- File names, saved locations, or window titles
- Personal text or content you create
- Your name, email address, or licensing information

Reports will generally be sent approximately once per day while Reelo is running and diagnostic reporting is enabled.

These reports do not use persistent identifiers to track individual installations. However, limited technical information associated with network connections may still be processed by service providers, so diagnostic information should not be considered completely anonymous.

## 4. How We Use Information

Information collected through diagnostics is used to:

- Improve Reelo's stability and performance
- Identify and resolve bugs
- Understand which features are useful/not useful
- Improve compatibility with different computers
- Make informed decisions about future improvements

We do not sell or rent diagnostic information.

We do not use diagnostic reports for targeted advertising, tracking your activity across other applications, or creating individual user profiles.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Reelo may use trusted third-party services to support limited functions, including delivering software resources, processing diagnostic reports, and hosting project information.

These providers may process basic technical information, such as your IP address, when your computer connects to their services.

Diagnostic analytics are intended to use European-hosted storage, although some information may be processed in other countries depending on the service provider.

We do not intentionally include your IP address in stored diagnostic reports or use it to create location-based user profiles.

Your personal recordings and media are not shared with these providers as part of Reelo's normal recording, editing, or AI processing features.

## 6. Your Privacy and Control

You remain in control of the content you create using Reelo.

You can choose what to record, manage your saved recordings, and delete files from your computer whenever you wish.

Reelo does not collect your personal recordings or media for analytics.

If you contact us for support, we may receive information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address, a description of your issue, or files you choose to share. We use this information to respond to your request.

You can contact us with questions or requests concerning personal information we may hold.

## 7. Data Storage and Security

Reelo stores your recordings and application preferences locally on your device.

Limited diagnostic information, when reporting is introduced, will be handled by external service providers for the purposes described in this policy.

We aim to limit the information collected to what is reasonably necessary to maintain and improve Reelo.

While we take reasonable steps to protect information handled through our services, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect improvements to Reelo, changes in how information is handled, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, we will update the date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this policy occasionally.
